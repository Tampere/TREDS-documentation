# Card

<ComponentBadges name="Card" lang="en" />

A card brings one thing's title, text, image and actions together on one surface. The card itself is not a link.

<ComponentLinks name="Card" lang="en" />

<Example name="card/Hero" lang="en" />

## When to use

Use cards to present parallel things, like services or locations, where each has its own title and a short description. A card can contain several links or buttons.

**Don't use a card when**

- the whole card must lead to one destination – use <ComponentRef name="Linkbox" lang="en" />
- you need just a surface without a heading structure – use <ComponentRef name="Paper" lang="en" />
- the content is long, continuous text. A card suits compact information.

## Variants and situations

### Title, text and actions

`title` is required, and `eyebrow` appears small above it. `children` goes below the title and `actions` at the very end of the card, as in the example at the top of the page.

### Image at the top

`media` shows an image at the top of the card in a 3:2 frame. The image is cropped to the frame, so keep the important content in the middle.

<Example name="card/MediaTop" lang="en" />

### Image on the left

`mediaPlacement="left"` puts the image and the content side by side, half each. Use it only in wide places.

<Example name="card/MediaLeft" lang="en" />

### Size

`size` is `lg` (default), `md` or `sm`. It sets the padding and the heading level: `lg` is H2, `md` and `sm` are H3. `titleOrder` changes the heading level so the cards fit the page's heading structure.

<Example name="card/Sizes" lang="en" />

### Coloured background

`background` is `turquoise`, `blue` or `pink`. The card turns its own text and links light. Use coloured backgrounds only for text and links.

<Example name="card/Background" lang="en" />

## Content guidelines

- Keep the title short and descriptive. It is the card's name for screen readers too.
- Keep the text to a couple of sentences. A card is an introduction, not the whole content.
- Use the same structure for parallel cards: images on all or none, the same size.
- Write button and link texts so they make sense outside the card: "Book a group room at Metso", or at least so the title names the target.

## Accessibility

**The component handles**

- The title is a real heading element (H2 or H3, or the `titleOrder` level), so screen reader users find cards by heading.
- The card's links and buttons stay separate focusable elements.
- On a coloured background the card's text and links turn light, and the link's focus ring is light.

**Your responsibility**

- Choose the heading level to fit the page structure with `size` or `titleOrder`. Don't skip levels.
- Give the image `alt` text if it tells something the title and text don't. Otherwise use `alt=""`.

WCAG criteria: [1.1.1 Non-text Content](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/non-text-content.html), [1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html), [1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html), [2.4.6 Headings and Labels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html)
