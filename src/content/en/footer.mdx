# Footer

<ComponentBadges name="Footer" lang="en" />

The footer is the bar at the bottom of the site: the copyright text, the accessibility statement and other legal links, and a link back to the top. The extended version also has contact details and social media links.

<ComponentLinks name="Footer" lang="en" />

<Example name="footer/Hero" lang="en" scroll />

The footer of this site is also a Footer.

## When to use

Use the footer on every page of every City of Tampere web service, always the same way. It is the page's `<footer>`, after the main content.

**Don't use a footer when**

- you need an in-page summary or an info box – use <ComponentRef name="Paper" lang="en" /> or <ComponentRef name="Card" lang="en" /> in the content

## Variants and situations

### Basic footer

The default version has the coat of arms, the copyright text, the legal links and a link back to the top, as in the example at the top of the page. The legal links always show in the same order: cookies (`cookies`), accessibility statement (`accessibilityStatement`), privacy (`privacy`), terms (`terms`) and finally `legalLinks`.

The accessibility statement is required, because the Finnish Act on the Provision of Digital Services (306/2019) requires one for every public-sector web service.

### Extended footer

`columns` adds the Tampere logo and content columns above the bar, for example contact details and a feedback link. In the columns, <ComponentRef name="Typography" lang="en" /> and <ComponentRef name="TextLink" lang="en" /> turn white on the blue background. `socialLinks` adds social media links next to the logo. They only show when `columns` is given.

<Example name="footer/Extended" lang="en" scroll />

### Dense footer

`variant="dense"` shows only the copyright text and the legal links, without the coat of arms and the back-to-top link. Use it for example in apps with short pages.

<Example name="footer/Dense" lang="en" scroll />

### Back to top

The link goes to `#top` by default, the start of the page, and also moves focus there. The next Tab then goes to the page's first link, for example a <ComponentRef name="SkipLink" lang="en" />. `backToTopHref` changes the target and `backToTop={false}` hides the link.

## Content guidelines

- Keep the legal links' texts short and familiar: "Accessibility statement", "Privacy", "Cookies".
- Put only information about the whole service in the columns, like contact details and feedback.
- Give the column headings a level that fits the page structure, usually `h2`.
- **The built-in texts are Finnish:** `Copyright © Tampereen kaupunki <year>` and "Sivun alkuun". On an English page, pass `copyrightText` and `backToTopLabel`.

## Accessibility

**The component handles**

- The footer is a `<footer>` landmark. The legal links and the social media links are lists.
- The social media links are named after the service, for example "Facebook". `label` changes the name.
- The coat of arms and the logo are hidden from screen readers.
- The back-to-top link moves focus to the start of the page, not just the scroll position.
- The links' focus ring is light, so it stands out on the blue background.

**Your responsibility**

- Give the correct address of the accessibility statement. The statement is specific to each service.
- Use headings in the columns that fit the page's heading structure.
- Say in a social media link's name if it doesn't lead to the city's main channel, for example "Tampere libraries on Instagram".

WCAG criteria: [1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html), [2.4.3 Focus Order](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/focus-order.html), [2.4.4 Link Purpose](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/link-purpose-in-context.html), [3.2.3 Consistent Navigation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/consistent-navigation.html)
