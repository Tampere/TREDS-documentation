# Footer

<ComponentBadges name="Footer" lang="en" />

The footer is the bar at the bottom of the site: the copyright text, the accessibility statement and other legal links, and a link back to the top. The extended version also has contact details and social media links.

<ComponentLinks name="Footer" lang="en" />

<Example name="footer/Hero" lang="en" scroll />

The footer of this site is also a Footer.

## When to use

Use the footer on every page of every City of Tampere web service, always the same way. It is the page's `<footer>`, after the main content.

**Don't use a footer when**

- you need an in-page summary or an info box – use <ComponentRef name="Paper" lang="en" /> or <ComponentRef name="Card" lang="en" /> in the content

## Variants and situations

### Basic footer

The legal links always show in the same order: cookies (`cookies`), accessibility statement (`accessibilityStatement`), privacy (`privacy`), terms (`terms`) and finally `legalLinks`. The accessibility statement is required, because the Finnish Act on the Provision of Digital Services (306/2019) requires one for every public-sector web service.

### Extended footer

`columns` adds the Tampere logo and content columns above the bar, for example contact details and a feedback link. In the columns, <ComponentRef name="Typography" lang="en" /> and <ComponentRef name="TextLink" lang="en" /> turn white on the blue background. `socialLinks` adds social media links next to the logo. They only show when `columns` is given.

<Example name="footer/Extended" lang="en" scroll />

### Dense footer

`variant="dense"` shows only the copyright text and the legal links, without the coat of arms and the back-to-top link. Use it for example in apps with short pages.

<Example name="footer/Dense" lang="en" scroll />

## Accessibility

- Give the correct address of the accessibility statement. The statement is specific to each service.
- Keep the legal links' texts short and familiar: "Accessibility statement", "Privacy", "Cookies".
- Put only information about the whole service in the columns, like contact details and feedback. Give the column headings a level that fits the page structure, usually `h2`.
- Say in a social media link's name if it doesn't lead to the city's main channel, for example "Tampere libraries on Instagram".
- The back-to-top link goes to `#top` by default and moves focus there, so the next Tab reaches the page's first link, for example a <ComponentRef name="SkipLink" lang="en" />. `backToTopHref` changes the target and `backToTop={false}` hides the link.
- The built-in texts are Finnish: `Copyright © Tampereen kaupunki <year>` and "Sivun alkuun". On an English page, pass `copyrightText` and `backToTopLabel`.
