# Tampere Design System

TREDS is the component library behind the City of Tampere's digital services: accessible, consistent building blocks for React, so every service looks and works like part of the same city. It builds on the Tampere brand's visual identity and is subordinate to it.

<StartCards lang="en" />

## Built on the Tampere brand

TREDS applies the City of Tampere's visual identity to digital services, and the brand comes first: colours, typography, logos and the coat of arms are defined by the <External href="https://www.tampere.fi/en/tampere-brand" lang="en">Tampere brand guidelines</External>, and TREDS follows them.
