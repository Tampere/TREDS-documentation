# Paper

<ComponentBadges name="Paper" lang="en" />

Paper is just a surface: background, corners, a shadow or border, and padding. It has no content structure or behaviour of its own.

<ComponentLinks name="Paper" lang="en" />

<Example name="paper/Hero" lang="en" />

## When to use

Use Paper when your own content needs a distinct surface and no ready-made component fits. <ComponentRef name="Card" lang="en" /> and <ComponentRef name="Linkbox" lang="en" /> are built on Paper.

**Don't use Paper when**

- the content has a title, text and maybe actions – use <ComponentRef name="Card" lang="en" />
- the whole surface must be a link – use <ComponentRef name="Linkbox" lang="en" />
- you only want to group form fields – use <ComponentRef name="Fieldset" lang="en" />

## Variants and situations

### Shadow or border

By default Paper has a shadow, as in the example at the top of the page. `withBorder` adds a border and `withShadow={false}` removes the shadow. `borderColor="brand"` makes the border brand blue.

<Example name="paper/Border" lang="en" />

### Padding

`padding` is `sm`, `md` (default), `lg` or `none`. The padding scales with the screen width.

<Example name="paper/Padding" lang="en" />

### Coloured background

`background` is `default` (white), `turquoise`, `blue` or `pink`. A coloured background doesn't change the text colour: TREDS text on them measures only 1.3–2.6:1 (#210). For text on a coloured background, use <ComponentRef name="Card" lang="en" />, which turns its text light.

### Rounded corners

`radius="pill"` rounds the corners fully. It suits only low surfaces, like a single line of text.

### Semantic element

Paper is a `<div>` by default. `component` changes the element, for example to `section` or `article`. Give a section a name with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` so screen readers treat it as a landmark.

## Accessibility

**The component handles**

- Paper adds no roles or behaviour. Its content is read as is.

**Your responsibility**

- The shadow and border are visual only. If the surface marks off a section of the page, use `component="section"` and give it a name.
- Don't use a coloured background with text until #210 is fixed. Check the contrast if the surface has content.
- A border in the `divider` colour alone is low-contrast. Don't rely on it as the only way to show where a section ends, if that matters.

WCAG criteria: [1.3.1 Info and Relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html), [1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)
